How many javelins should you sell before you hop worlds?

The vendor pays 96 gp for your first adamant javelin and 240 gp for your first rune one — but every javelin you sell on a world knocks 0.1% off the base value of the next. This calculator prices out a full run and finds the batch size that leaves you with the most gold.

Step 3 · the workings

How the numbers are made

No black box. Every figure on this page comes from the shop's own pricing rule and the two inputs above.

Price paid for the i-th javelin sold on a world

price(i) = ⌊ base × (60% − 0.1% × i) ⌋

Where

i = 0 for the first javelin, 1 for the second, and so on base = 160 gp (adamant) or 400 gp (rune)

Revenue on a world is the sum of that price over the batch. Profit is revenue minus what you paid, and a fresh world resets i back to zero.

Heads up

—

  1. World hopping is the reset. Each world starts with 500 javelins in stock and pays full price again, while waiting only returns 1 adamant javelin every 48s (1 rune every 1m). Hopping beats waiting by a mile.
  2. Prices round down to whole gp. The shop never pays a fraction of a coin, so each step is floored before it's added up.
  3. Time is yours to set. Gold per hour uses the seconds-per-hop and seconds-per-sell-X values you entered, and assumes one sell-X click moves 50 javelins.
  4. The last world can be partial. If your total doesn't divide evenly by the batch size, the final world sells whatever is left at full starting price.
  5. GE prices move. The default buy-in is the shop table's listed GE price, but the margin is only real if your purchase price is entered — that single number swings the verdict more than anything else.
